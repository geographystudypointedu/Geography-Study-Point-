Introduction

Geo Study Point is an independent educational technology platform whose core educational resources are intended to remain free of charge. The platform is intended to support students, including learners from rural and resource-constrained communities, by providing accessible educational practice resources without requiring financial contribution as a condition of access.

It is designed to facilitate academic practice, interactive examinations, automated evaluations, result generation, and AI-assisted learning. By accessing, browsing, or using this platform, you expressly acknowledge, understand, and agree to comply with the following comprehensive terms and policies.

1. Privacy Policy

1.1 Legal Reference & Scope

This framework is designed with reference to applicable provisions of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025, applicable Information Technology laws and regulations, and relevant international digital governance & security principles.

1.2 Categories of Personal Data Collected

  • Identification Data: Full name, registered email address, and telephone/mobile numbers.
  • Academic & Institutional Data: Class, course name, academic year, and educational details.
  • Examination & Operational Data: Unique Examination Attempt IDs, Verification IDs, submitted answers, grades, scores, and active timestamps.
  • Technical & Device Data: IP addresses, browser types, device information, authentication tokens, cookies, and system log files generated during platform usage.

3. Security, Terms & Responsible Use

3.1 Account & Administrative Security

Users are strictly prohibited from bypassing authentication gates or attempting unauthorized database tampering.

3.2 Examination Integrity & AI Misuse

Students must undertake examinations independently, relying solely on personal knowledge. Such conduct as tampering with timers or session hijacking may result in disqualification.

4. Voluntary Support, Payment & Maintenance

4.1 Voluntary Nature & Open Access

Financial contributions or support offered by users are entirely voluntary (স্বেচ্ছাসেবী) and optional. No student will ever be barred from accessing education due to an inability to contribute financially.

4.2 Strict Separation of Support and Academic Grades

Voluntary support is not a purchase of educational services, marks, examination outcomes or guaranteed platform availability. Financial contributions do not buy grades, grant grace marks, or influence academic evaluations in any manner whatsoever.
