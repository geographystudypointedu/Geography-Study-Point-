Policy & Information Centre

Data Minimization & Processing: This platform processes limited student data strictly for educational and examination verification purposes. Temporary files uploaded for evaluation are securely processed and deleted following assessment. We strictly adhere to core principles of modern Data Protection architectures, prioritizing your privacy at all times.
IP & Architecture Ownership: The complete architecture, UI/UX design, custom backend workflows, and examination question sets are the intellectual property of Geo Study Point and its Founder, Mr. Swastik Halder. Unauthorized scraping, replication, mirroring, or commercial exploitation is strictly prohibited.
Security by Design: Security is an ongoing responsibility. We employ layered backend validation and explicit authorization protocols (Zero Trust approach). Students are required to maintain examination integrity. Any attempt to manipulate API endpoints, bypass session timers, or execute unauthorized requests will result in automated auditing and session termination.
Voluntary Operations: Core educational access on Geo Study Point is 100% non-commercial and free. Financial contributions from users are strictly voluntary and assist exclusively with server maintenance and backend infrastructure costs. Support contributions hold no influence over academic grades, AI evaluation, or official platform results.
